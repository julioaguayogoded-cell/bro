MISSION

A new way to connect for the healthy development of children and teenagers.

BroS Life is a family of devices with its own operating system for children and teenagers aged 8 to 16. It turns them from passive consumers into active creators.

THE PROBLEM

We give them an adult phone earlier and earlier. And it is designed to hook them.

The question is no longer whether a child will have a phone, but which phone. Today there is only the adult one, with infinite feeds and strangers. Parental controls arrive late and do not change what happens inside.

And families are moving: in Spain they organise to delay the first phone and have taken petitions to Parliament, and the EU is legislating to protect minors online. What is missing is an alternative.
THE SOLUTION

A phone and a wristband built from scratch for this age, with an app for parents. No likes, no feeds, no strangers. With Bro AI, which makes them think and never decides for the child.

OUR MISSION WITH AI

AI will be part of every child’s life. From day one, our mission is for it to arrive in a way that is ethical and safe for their development: an AI that helps them think and create, never to depend on it or stop deciding for themselves.
